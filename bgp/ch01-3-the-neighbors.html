The Neighbors

Chapter 1: One Login, the Whole Internet · 3 November 2026 · Ioannis Alexander Konstas

Snapshot note. The session in this chapter was captured on 17 October 2021 at 17:33. The figures describe the Internet as it was then; the method is unchanged. For a current session (3 November 2026), and how the numbers have moved in five years, see Chapter 2, Part 8.

Below the header, each line is one BGP session. The columns that matter:

ColumnMeaning
ASThe neighbor's autonomous system
MsgRcvd / MsgSentBGP messages received and sent on this session
TblVerThe table version this neighbor has been brought up to
InQ / OutQMessages waiting to be processed or sent (backlog)
Up/DownHow long the session has been in its current state
State/PfxRcdA number means Established, with that many prefixes; a word means not established
