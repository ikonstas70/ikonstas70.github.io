MailBlock: Stop Unwanted Email, Safely

Articles · November 2026 · Ioannis (John) Konstas

Every business on Microsoft 365 knows this inbox: the same sender every day, "unsubscribe" ignored, and blocking them in the admin portal is a job for an expert. MailBlock turns that into one private page: type a sender or a whole domain, press Add, and it is blocked across the entire mail system in seconds. Press remove, and it is unblocked just as fast. All names shown here are examples, not real customers.

Why a careless block is dangerous

Spammers forge sender addresses all the time. A tool that bounces everything will send its bounces to innocent people whose addresses were forged, and the business that sent them starts to look like a spam source. Bounces also help attackers learn which addresses exist. A block has to be smart about who gets an answer.

The smart bounce

  • Genuine sender, proven by Microsoft: a polite, final bounce, "The email account that you tried to reach is no longer active", so they remove you from their list.
  • Forged or unproven: silence. The message is held for your review and nobody receives a reply, so your company can never be used as a weapon.
  • Generic text: no names, no internal details, nothing an attacker can use.

Everything inside

  • Private dashboard: one page, one click, runs inside your business.
  • Smart bounce and silent quarantine, as described above.
  • Attempt tracker: how many times each blocked sender tried, and when, with every attempt one click away.
  • Status check: whether any sender's latest email arrived, bounced, or is being held.
  • Secure connection: encrypted to today's strongest, post-quantum-ready standard.
  • Automatic sync: every change reaches Exchange Online by itself.
  • Backups and history: a backup before every change, and a permanent record of every action.
  • Always on: it starts by itself and keeps running.

Secure by design

  • Private: nothing is exposed to the internet.
  • Least privilege: it manages blocking only and cannot read anyone's mail.
  • Reversible: every change can be undone in one click.
  • Built by an auditor: security is part of the design, not an afterthought.
